About Us

Welcome to Cold Cuts Project PH — where every gathering becomes an unforgettable experience through beautifully curated grazing tables and custom food stations.

Based in Calumpit, Central Luzon, we specialize in crafting stunning spreads filled with premium cheeses, artisan cold cuts, fresh fruits, gourmet dips, and handcrafted sweets.

Whether it's a birthday, wedding, corporate event, baby shower, or any celebration, we bring flavor, style, and quality together — so you can focus on celebrating.
